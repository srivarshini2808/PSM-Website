Our process

Simple on the surface. Disciplined underneath.

PSM structures each enquiry around the buyer's requirement, then coordinates sourcing and commercial details through clear stages.

01

Requirement

Receive the material category, specification, quantity, destination and other relevant details.

02

Sourcing

Evaluate sourcing possibilities against the stated technical and commercial requirement.

03

Evaluation

Review the proposed material information and commercial fit with the customer.

04

Commercial Coordination

Align quotation, documentation, terms and fulfilment details for the enquiry.

05

Delivery / Fulfilment

Coordinate the agreed next steps through the applicable supply arrangement.

What helps us respond well

Clear specifications create better sourcing conversations.

  • MaterialCategory and requested material details.
  • SpecificationGrade, technical requirements or other defining information.
  • QuantityRequired quantity and preferred pack or shipment format where relevant.
  • DestinationDelivery or destination information relevant to the enquiry.
